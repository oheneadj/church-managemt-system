# Church Admin Onboarding Guide

> This guide is for the person setting up your church's account. You only need to do this once.

---

## What You'll Need Before You Start

- Your church's official name
- Your bank account details (for receiving digital offerings)
- Your phone number (this is your login — no password required)
- 10 minutes

---

## Step 1 — Sign Up

Go to **yourapp.com/signup** and enter:

1. **Church name** — Use the official name (e.g. "Grace Assembly Accra"). This becomes your subdomain.
2. **Your name** — You'll be the first admin.
3. **Your phone number** — You'll receive a one-time code on this number. This is how you log in every time.
4. **Church size** — Approximate number of regular attendees. This helps us recommend the right plan.

You'll receive a 6-digit code by SMS within 30 seconds. Enter it to continue.

---

## Step 2 — Your Church Subdomain

After signup, your church gets a private URL:

```
grace-assembly.yourapp.com
```

Bookmark this. Every staff member you add will use this same URL. The subdomain is based on your church name and can't be changed later, so make sure the name you entered is correct.

---

## Step 3 — Set Up Digital Giving (Optional but Recommended)

To accept offerings via MoMo (MTN, Telecel, AirtelTigo) or card, you need to connect a bank account:

1. Go to **Settings → Giving → Bank Account**
2. Enter your bank name and account number
3. We'll verify the account with Paystack (takes about 30 seconds)
4. Once verified, digital giving is enabled immediately

> **Why we need this:** When someone gives digitally, the money goes directly to your bank account. We take a small platform fee from each transaction. You don't pay anything upfront.

If you skip this step, you can still record cash offerings — you just won't be able to accept digital payments until the bank account is connected.

---

## Step 4 — Add Your Staff

Go to **Settings → Team → Invite Staff Member**.

Enter their name, phone number, and role:

| Role | What they can do |
|---|---|
| **Church Admin** | Everything — full access |
| **Pastor** | View members, attendance, giving summaries, send broadcasts |
| **Finance Secretary** | Record and view all contributions, view reports, export |
| **Department Head** | View members and attendance |
| **Youth Pastor** | View members and attendance |

They'll receive an SMS with a link to your church's URL. When they visit, they enter their phone number to log in — no password needed.

> **Recommendation:** Add at least two church admins (yourself + one other trusted person) so you're not locked out if you change your phone number.

---

## Step 5 — Import Your Members

If you have a list of members in Excel or on paper, you can add them in bulk.

**Option A — Bulk import from CSV:**
1. Go to **Members → Import**
2. Download the CSV template
3. Fill in the template with your member data
4. Upload it

Required columns: `first_name`, `last_name`, `phone` (optional but strongly recommended)

**Option B — Add members one by one:**
Go to **Members → Add Member** and fill in the form.

**Option C — Start fresh and add as you go:**
You don't need to import everyone before you start. You can add members over time.

---

## Step 6 — Enable WhatsApp CHECKIN (Optional)

WhatsApp CHECKIN lets members check themselves in to a service by sending "CHECKIN" to your church's WhatsApp number.

> ⚠️ WhatsApp CHECKIN requires our platform to be registered with Meta Business. This is handled on our end — you don't need a Meta account. It is available on the Standard and Pro plans.

To enable:
1. Go to **Settings → Attendance → WhatsApp CHECKIN**
2. Toggle it on
3. Share the platform WhatsApp number with your congregation (we'll show it to you here)

Members need to save the number and message it at least once for WhatsApp to allow us to message them back.

---

## Step 7 — Configure SMS Receipts

By default, when a member gives (cash or digital), they receive an SMS receipt. This is one of the most appreciated features.

Your plan includes a monthly SMS credit allowance. Check **Settings → SMS → Credits** to see your balance.

You can:
- **Disable cash receipts** — if you prefer not to use credits for this
- **Top up your credits** — if you run low (buy credit bundles in the settings)
- **Preview your credit balance** before sending a broadcast

---

## You're Ready

Your church is set up. Here's a quick summary of what you can do:

**Before Sunday:**
- Create a service record: **Attendance → Services → New Service**
- Open it for CHECKIN on Sunday morning: **Open for Check-in**

**On Sunday:**
- Members CHECKIN via WhatsApp, or staff check them in manually
- Finance secretary records offerings as they come in

**After Sunday:**
- Close the service: **Close Service**
- View the Sunday report: **Giving → Sunday Report**
- Export the attendance list if needed

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Can I change my church's subdomain later?**
No — the subdomain is permanent. If you made a typo in your church name, contact support.

**What happens if I lose my phone?**
Contact another church admin and ask them to update your phone number. If you're the only admin, contact our support line.

**Can two staff members be logged in at the same time?**
Yes — multiple people can be logged in simultaneously from different devices.

**Is member data safe?**
Yes. Each church's data is completely separate. Staff from another church cannot see your members, giving records, or any other data. Sensitive information (like national ID numbers) is encrypted.

**How do I cancel?**
Contact support. Your data is exportable at any time — you're never locked in.
